---
title: Cellulose-Graphene Oxide Composite Beads (GOCB) for Lead Remediation
affiliation: Dhaka University Nanotechnology Centre — Research Fellowship
period: February 2025 – November 2025
order: 1
---

Under a DU Nanotechnology Centre research fellowship, this project developed a novel nanocomposite adsorbent for removing lead (Pb²⁺) from industrial wastewater. Cellulose nanocrystals from waste cotton clothes were combined with graphene oxide from recycled pencil batteries via a sol-gel technique to produce GOCB beads, characterised using FTIR, XRD, FESEM, TEM and DLS. The Freundlich isotherm model (R² = 0.9932) described the adsorption, with a maximum capacity of 64.94 mg/g; chemisorption was the dominant mechanism, higher GO content gave greater Pb²⁺ removal, and the beads showed no significant performance loss across regeneration cycles. The resulting paper has been submitted for publication.
