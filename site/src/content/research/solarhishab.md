As the final project of the Shoktikonna Leadership Cohort 2025, Oishee developed and pitch-presented SolarHishab, an interactive tool designed to estimate electricity savings and return on investment for urban rooftop solar installations. The project aimed to encourage uptake among urban consumers in Bangladesh by making the economic case visible and accessible.

## About the project

SolarHishab started with a question: why are solar panels on rooftops staying unused? The reasons include limited technical support, uncertainty about reliability, high upfront costs and lack of awareness, so the team focused on showcasing the benefits. By entering a few details, users see how much they will save on their electricity bill and how many years it takes to get the upfront cost returned. The team proposed integrating it with bill-payment apps such as DESCO or bKash: after paying a bill, a message pops up, "You are paying extra by not using solar panels; see how much you can save."
