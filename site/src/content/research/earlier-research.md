Prior to the nanotechnology fellowship, Oishee contributed to the manuscripts of two published studies:

- Cotton gauze modification using Cynodon dactylon (Bermuda grass) extract, assessing chemical and antimicrobial properties for sustainable medical textile applications. Published in Scientific Reports (2024), it found strong antimicrobial activity against S. aureus and negligible skin irritation, proposing the gauze as a sustainable medical bandage material.
- Plant-based dye for silk using Crape Myrtle flower extract, optimising performance and mordanting technique. Published in Results in Materials (2026).
